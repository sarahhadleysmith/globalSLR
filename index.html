    Goal: Keep your village alive — prevent homes from flooding.

    Orange boxes = villager homes  |  Green boxes = gardens

    Each surviving garden produces 1 food per turn. Flooded gardens produce nothing.

    Your villager's action (pick one per turn):
      B = Make 1 brick   F = Gather 1 food   P = Plant a mangrove (costs 2 food, slows the sea)

    Shared wall actions (any player, any time):
      Build Wall = raise wall height (costs 3 bricks)
      Move Wall Left / Right = reposition the wall (costs 1 brick each)

    All players must click End Turn to advance the round.

    You lose if all homes flood or food reaches 0.
